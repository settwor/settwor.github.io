{%- comment -%} Opens the biography in the language of the visitor. - Links marked data-bio-link (Biography in the menus) lead to the visitor's language. - The language the visitor picks in the language list is remembered. - The address of the English biography sends visitors with another browser language to their own biography. Other biography pages are never redirected, so people who come from Google in a given language always get that language. Ukrainian browsers get the Ukrainian biography (since 2.4). If a Ukrainian file is ever missing they get English, never Russian. The list of languages comes from the folder _bio, so a new language works at once. {%- endcomment -%}
