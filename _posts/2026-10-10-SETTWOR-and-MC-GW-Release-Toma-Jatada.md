On October 10, 2026, SETTWOR released [*Toma Jatada*](https://open.spotify.com/album/00W2qdOjV3whkK3L6W2VxL), a collaboration with Brazilian artist [MC GW](https://open.spotify.com/artist/0f1IECbrVV952unZkzrsg2). Authentic Records supported the release. It is SETTWOR’s next track in [Brazilian funk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Funk_carioca).[\[1\]](#ref-1)[\[3\]](#ref-3)

MC GW, whose real name is Gláucio William da Silva Jerônimo, is a singer and songwriter from [São João de Meriti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S%C3%A3o_Jo%C3%A3o_de_Meriti), in [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro_(state)). He started out performing [samba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samba) and later moved into the Brazilian funk scene. He is known for his vocal style and for the number of artists he has worked with.[\[2\]](#ref-2)[\[4\]](#ref-4)[\[5\]](#ref-5)[\[6\]](#ref-6)

His career picked up with *Sarra Novinha no Grau*. *Ritmo Mexicano* and *Atura ou Surta* came after it. His vocals now appear outside Brazil too, in Brazilian funk, phonk and electronic remixes. One example is *MONTAGEM - PR FUNK*, released in 2023 with S3BZS and MC Menor do Alvorada. Producers in many different styles use his vocals.

With *Toma Jatada*, SETTWOR joins the artists who have worked with MC GW. He began experimenting with Brazilian funk in [late 2025](/releases.html#release-noite-selvagem). All of his tracks are listed on the [releases page](/releases.html).[\[7\]](#ref-7)
