SETTWOR has started planning and building a record label, Nutaria Records. The work began on November 1, 2026. Before that day there were only plans.

The name comes from the Polish word *nuta*, which means "note".

The label's website, [nutaria.settwor.com.pl](https://nutaria.settwor.com.pl), is already online and working.[\[1\]](#ref-1) For now it is empty. No launch date has been set, and the launch may be a long way off.

The genres are not decided yet. At the moment they are phonk and Brazilian Phonk.

SETTWOR does not want to rush it. He plans to prepare as much as possible before the label opens, so that it does not turn out to be one more low-quality label. This preparation is what he is working on now.

One of his aims is great respect for the label's artists and for their tracks. The planning is built around it.
