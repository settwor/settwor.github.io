**October 29, 2026.** SETTWOR has purchased a new domain, and the website is moving to it. The main site now lives at **[settwor.com.pl](http://settwor.com.pl)**, and every project is being brought under the same roof.

**New Addresses**

- **Main website:** [settwor.com.pl](http://settwor.com.pl)
- **CDRA:** [cdra.settwor.com.pl](http://cdra.settwor.com.pl)
- **Ledgertone:** [ledgertone.settwor.com.pl](http://ledgertone.settwor.com.pl)
- **All projects:** settwor.com.pl/projects

The full list of projects is always available at ++[settwor.com.pl/projects](https://settwor.com.pl/projects)++.

**Why the Move**

Until now, SETTWOR's website and projects were spread across separate addresses. Moving everything to one domain makes the whole ecosystem easier to find, easier to remember, and clearer to visitors: one name, one place, every project connected to it.

**What Happens to the Old Links**

Old addresses are being replaced by the new ones. Visitors who use the new addresses listed above will always reach the current version of each site. If you have bookmarked or shared an old link, please update it to the new address.

**What Comes Next**

The new domain is not only a change of address but a base for what follows. New SETTWOR projects will launch on the same domain, as subdomains such as [project.settwor.com.pl](http://project.settwor.com.pl), and will appear on the ++[projects page](https://settwor.com.pl/projects)++ as they are released.

CDRA, which returned on October 27, and Ledgertone, launched this month, are the first two projects on the new domain.

*Follow the news section for updates on new projects and releases.*
