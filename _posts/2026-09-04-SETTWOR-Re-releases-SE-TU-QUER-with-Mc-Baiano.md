On September 4, 2026, [SE TU QUER](https://open.spotify.com/album/4G27W8LQxr3H8mROcotpUQ) was re-released through Authentic Records as a collaboration between SETTWOR and Mc Baiano.[\[1\]](#ref-1)

Authentic Records had contacted SETTWOR because the original version used Mc Baiano’s vocals without crediting him as a featured artist. The sides discussed it and agreed on a new version with Mc Baiano credited.

It is SETTWOR’s first collaboration with another artist in [five years of releasing music](/news/2026/10/SETTWOR-Celebrates-Five-Years-of-Music/).[\[2\]](#ref-2)[\[3\]](#ref-3) The track is listed among [all SETTWOR releases](/releases.html#release-se-tu-quer).
