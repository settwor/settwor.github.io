On September 4, 2026, [SE TU QUER](https://open.spotify.com/album/4G27W8LQxr3H8mROcotpUQ) was re-released through Authentic Records, this time as an official collaboration between SETTWOR and Mc Baiano.[\[1\]](#ref-1)

The re-release came after Authentic Records contacted SETTWOR about using Mc Baiano’s vocals in the original version without crediting him as a featured artist. The situation was discussed, and the parties agreed to release a new version officially crediting Mc Baiano as a collaborator.

The release marks SETTWOR’s first collaboration with another artist in [five years of his musical career](/news/2026/10/SETTWOR-Celebrates-Five-Years-of-Music/).[\[2\]](#ref-2)[\[3\]](#ref-3) What began as a discussion about vocal credits ultimately led to an official collaboration and a new release of SE TU QUER. See it among [all SETTWOR releases](/releases.html#release-se-tu-quer).
