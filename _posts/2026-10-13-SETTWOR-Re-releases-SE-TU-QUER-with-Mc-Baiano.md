On September 4, 2026, SE TU QUER was re-released through Authentic Records, this time as an official collaboration between SETTWOR and Mc Baiano.

The re-release came after Authentic Records contacted SETTWOR about using Mc Baiano’s vocals in the original version without crediting him as a featured artist. The situation was discussed, and the parties agreed to release a new version officially crediting Mc Baiano as a collaborator.

The release marks SETTWOR’s first collaboration with another artist in five years of his musical career. What began as a discussion about vocal credits ultimately led to an official collaboration and a new release of SE TU QUER.
