The official website. From Kryvyi Rih, Ukraine, based in Poland, active since 2021.

About SETTWOR

SETTWOR is a music producer specializing in phonk, Brazilian funk, and dance/electronic music. Originally from Kryvyi Rih, Ukraine, he relocated to Poland in 2022.

He is best known for his music, his YouTube channel, and short TikTok videos featuring his tracks. He is also the owner of one of the popular 8D music channels.

SETTWOR is one of the most popular phonk artists on Pandora.
