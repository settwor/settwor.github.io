About SETTWOR

SETTWOR (Daniil Shulha), also known as Setwor, Сеттвор and Сетвор, is a music producer specializing in phonk, Brazilian funk, and dance/electronic music. Originally from Kryvyi Rih, Ukraine, he relocated to Poland in 2022.

He is best known for his music, his YouTube channel, and short TikTok videos featuring his tracks. He also runs an 8D music channel. His music is available on major streaming platforms, including Spotify and Pandora.
